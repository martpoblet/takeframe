Top quality

Each take is recorded in full on the iPhone, in 4K and HDR (ProRes and Apple Log on Pro models), and moves to your Mac without re-encoding.

Full control from your Mac

Lens, zoom, manual focus, ISO, shutter, white balance and torch. Save your settings as scenes and get back to them in one click.

Audio in sync

Your Mac's microphone is joined to the video, aligned by sound to the millisecond. No clapping needed.

Professional monitor

Near-instant live preview with histogram, zebras, focus peaking, grid and horizon level.

Teleprompter

Your script right next to the camera. It starts and stops with the recording.

A lighter iPhone

Once a take is safely on your Mac, it's deleted from the iPhone to free up space.
